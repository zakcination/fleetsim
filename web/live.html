Same algorithms as the fleetsim v0.1 Python package, ported to run in your browser: the League of Robot Runners warehouse crop (72 × 99 cells, 2,920 pod slots, 19 stations), Poisson orders, full-errand space-time A* with a reservation table, and three dispatch policies. One tick = one second. KPIs use a rolling 10-minute window of simulated time.
